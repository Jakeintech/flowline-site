2. What Flowline is

Flowline is a single-player puzzle-strategy game. A volcano erupts on a small island; you build walls and dig trenches to steer the lava away from your villages and into the sea, where it cools into land you can settle. It has no account of its own. The Daily Island mode has an optional leaderboard through Apple's Game Center.
