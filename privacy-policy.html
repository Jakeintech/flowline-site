Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

The short version.

  • Flowline collects no data. Its App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected".
  • No account, no ads, no in-app purchases, and no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting software.
  • Your runs, best scores and settings stay on your iPhone.
  • The game has no server of its own. The optional Daily Island leaderboard uses Apple's Game Center; other players on it see your Game Center display name and score.

1. Who we are

Flowline is a game for iPhone made by Jake Williams ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles, where it goes and the choices you have. Questions: info@jakeawilliams.com.

3. No server of its own

Flowline has no server and no backend, and contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting software. Every mode works without a connection. The only online feature is the Daily Island leaderboard, which Apple's Game Center provides: your score travels from your iPhone to Game Center, never to us.

4. Permissions

Flowline asks for no permissions. It does not use your location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, health data or notifications. It does not track you across other companies' apps and websites, so it never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

5. Game Center

Game Center is used only for the Daily Island leaderboard. Apple runs Game Center and processes that information under its own privacy policy. Flowline does not receive your Apple Account, email address or contacts. If you are not signed in, the game plays exactly the same and simply keeps your daily best on the device. You control your Game Center profile and visibility in Settings > Game Center.

6. What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in of our own.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No third-party analytics, tracking or crash-reporting software.
  • No chat, and nothing shared between players beyond a Game Center score.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Your runs, scores and settings stay on your iPhone until you delete the game, which removes everything Flowline stored. Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

To delete it, touch and hold Flowline on the Home Screen, tap Remove App, then Delete App. Copies inside your own device backups, in iCloud or on a computer, follow the rules of that backup. Scores recorded in Game Center are managed by Apple.

8. Children

Flowline is rated 4+ on the App Store. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Game Center is controlled by Apple and can be restricted by a parent with Screen Time.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data. Flowline keeps your data on your own iPhone, so you can exercise these rights directly by deleting the game. We do not sell or share personal information. If you have a question about your rights, write to info@jakeawilliams.com.

10. This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics, tracking or third-party resources; its fonts and images are served from the site itself. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under the GitHub privacy statement.

11. Security

Flowline relies on the protections built into iOS: its saves sit inside the app's sandbox, protected by your passcode and the device's encryption.

12. App Store privacy label

Flowline's privacy label on the App Store reads Data Not Collected, which matches this policy. If you ever find the two disagree, tell us and we will correct it.

13. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. For a significant change we will also say so in the game.
