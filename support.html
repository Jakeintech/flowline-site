Playing

How does a turn work?

You get 2 actions a turn. Tap land to build a wall (1). Tap your wall again to dig it into a trench (1 in all). Tap lava to cool it into rock (2). Tap new black coast to found a house (1). The turn ends on its own shortly after your last action, or earlier with Pass turn. Then the lava moves one tile per front.

Where will the lava go?

Follow the dashed cyan arrows: each lava front moves into its lowest neighbour that is no higher than itself. A wall adds two levels of height, a trench takes one away. The arrows update the moment you build, so you can see the new path before the turn ends.

What do "Pooling" and "In danger" mean?

"Pooling" means a front has nowhere lower to go, so its tile rises instead; after two turns it cracks the walls next to it, after three it sets into rock. "In danger" (with a red ring) means a front will enter a village next turn unless you change its path.

How do I score?

Safe villages grow each turn: a house (50) becomes a hamlet (100) after two turns and a town (200) after four. Every tile of new land is worth 10. Lava that reaches the sea makes new land you can settle, so steering it to the coast is how you expand.

What is the red dashed tile with an X?

A lava bomb will land there next turn and start a new front. It aims for the cell next to your most valuable village. A wall on that tile is destroyed when the bomb lands.

Can I undo?

Yes. Anything you built this turn can be undone by tapping it again, and your actions are refunded. Once the turn ends, the lava has moved and the board stands.

Modes and the Daily Island

What are Run, Daily Island and Endless?

A Run is 5 islands, each harder, with an upgrade to pick after each. Daily Island is one island with the same layout for everyone that day, with a Game Center leaderboard. Endless keeps going until an island is lost.

When does the Daily Island change?

At midnight UTC. The leaderboard resets at the same time, so everyone plays the same island for the same 24 hours.

Do I need Game Center?

No. Every mode plays the same without it. Signing in to Game Center (Settings > Game Center on your iPhone) only adds the Daily Island leaderboard.

Do upgrades carry between runs?

No. Upgrades last for the rest of the run you picked them in. Each new run starts fresh.

Device and settings

How do I turn off sound, music or haptics?

Tap the gear on the title screen. Sound effects, music and haptics each have a switch.

What accessibility options are there?

Flowline follows the system Reduce Motion setting and also has its own switch under the gear on the title screen; it removes camera shake and slow motion and shortens animations. The HUD supports Dynamic Type. Colour is never the only signal: preview arrows have arrowheads, danger has a ring and a word, and bombs have an X.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone with iOS 18 or later. The game plays in portrait, one-handed.

Saves

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your iPhone. Flowline has no account and no server, so there is no copy anywhere else, and deleting the app deletes your progress.

I closed the app mid-run. Is it lost?

No. A run in progress is saved after every turn. Reopen the game and choose Continue run or Continue Endless on the title screen.

Purchase and privacy

Are there in-app purchases or ads?

No. Flowline is a premium game: you pay once on the App Store, and there are no ads and no in-app purchases.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the game: touch and hold Flowline on the Home Screen, tap Remove App, then Delete App. That removes your runs, scores and settings. We hold no copy, so there is nothing else to delete. The privacy policy has more.

What data does Flowline collect?

None. Your progress is saved on your iPhone, and the Daily Island leaderboard runs through Apple's Game Center. The privacy policy has the detail.
